# Historical ten-observation model-supported quality review

Use outputs/review_v2/ as the current reviewed dataset. Keep review_v1 and values_v2
as the reproducible baseline; do not combine their old evidence/counts with review_v2.
This document preserves the earlier ten-observation model review, whose findings are incorporated into the current human-reviewed Part 1 dataset and Part1_Summary.pdf.

## What was reviewed
AAPL 2024, ABT 2020, CVX 2024, JPM 2024, MSFT 2024, GOOGL 2024, NVDA 2022,
WFC 2024, TSLA 2024, and COP 2024. The assistant read extracted blocks and theme
evidence, checked archived HTML, and verified every retained review evidence passage
against HTML text and metadata checksums. The sample is purposive, not random.
This earlier model-supported quality check was not a human accuracy estimate. Nine observations were retained; Abbott 2020 was excluded. The project owner later confirmed human review was completed without further changes, and the active dataset now records final selection of accepted sources.

## Corrections and decisions
- Apple: retain five categories; exclude employee testimonials and recruitment navigation.
- Abbott: exclude 2020 because products, awards and exit notices dominate. Do not
  generalize the exclusion to other years, but inspect their similar templates later.
- Chevron: recover three actual div.body paragraphs missed by the extractor. Add
  inclusion, integrity, people and safety evidence. Recovered text is stored with
  review-specific IDs and verified against the archived HTML, not generated by a model.
- JPMorgan: retain eight themes; internal safety/risk culture is not a privacy claim.
  Remove trailing directory/exit notice and the CEO quotation from review text.
- Microsoft: add customer enablement and safety from explicit existing passages;
  retain the caveat that COVID-related messaging was displayed, not newly authored, in 2024.
- Alphabet: remove people label based on entrepreneur/CEO autonomy rather than clear
  employee wellbeing. Preserve historical-letter caveat and broad aspirational community claim.
- NVIDIA: add integrity based on candor. Remove environment because the only support
  is ambiguous sustainability recognition, not a clear environmental commitment.
- Wells Fargo: retain community/customer commitments in substantive blurbs. The earlier
  financial-navigation performance label removal remains in effect.
- Tesla: retain environment/inclusion; remove screen-reader download instructions from
  evidence and repeated product/administrative blocks from text. Inclusion is recruitment-scoped.
- ConocoPhillips: add explicit employee inclusion; remove article headlines. The corporate
  values blockquote is not assumed to be an employee testimonial merely due to its HTML tag.

## Outputs and interpretation
450 rows; 140 eligible provisional observations across 37 companies. 131 observations
remain model-coded outside this sample; nine are AI-reviewed provisional; one newly
excluded. The earlier 23 coding failures and 10 comparison failures remain unresolved.
Nine formerly known comparisons were invalidated because one reviewed input was
edited or excluded. They are not converted to unchanged. 83 known comparisons remain:
53 changed and 30 unchanged. Old comparisons are retained in comparison_details.json
under pre_review_comparison where applicable. No new API calls were made.

Outputs include the completed ai_quality_review.csv, updated CSV/JSON dataset and
theme_evidence.csv, decision audit, coverage, comparison details and summary. The old
human_review_worksheet.csv remains blank; use ai_quality_review.csv to describe this
review. Do not report it as human annotation. Metadata/language descriptions not directly
edited remain model output; they are not re-estimated by this review.

## Install and reproduce
Merge this package into the existing project. Outputs are already generated; no run
is required. To reproduce offline from existing batch_v1, values_v2 and review_v1:

    python -m part1_stated_values.src.apply_ai_review

Only Python's standard library is used. Exact reviewed decisions are in
config/ai_review_decisions.json (inside part1_stated_values). Evidence and source hashes
are checked. Source files and earlier outputs are not overwritten. ZIP includes only
new code, decisions and outputs, not duplicate raw archives.

## Remaining limitations
The strict body-only validator caused known missing-heading cases; recovered Chevron
text is a targeted correction, not a general extraction fix. Similar omissions may
remain in the other 440 observations. Recruitment scope, old letters, awards, boilerplate,
nonrandom archive availability and model errors limit inference. Early years contain
only six eligible observations each. Do not interpret raw yearly counts as population
trends, and never treat missing data as zero values or misalignment. Use sensitivity
analyses and report matched coverage in Parts 2–4. This completes the requested ten-row
model-supported review, preceded the completed human source review.
